Accuracy & no warranty

  • The playground does not run OpenFGA. Its resolver is an independent re-implementation in JavaScript of the publicly documented modeling language and query semantics, written for teaching.
  • Its answers, traces, “why not” explanations, suggested fixes and lint findings may differ from a real OpenFGA server, especially in edge cases, limits, condition evaluation and newer features.
  • Do not use it to make, verify or audit real authorization decisions. Test models against an actual OpenFGA server before relying on them.
  • The site and its content are provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. To the extent permitted by law, the author is not liable for any loss arising from its use.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking. The site sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Your data stays in your browser. Models, tuples, assertions and settings you create are processed locally and saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not sent to the site or to anyone else.
  • Deleting it: use “Delete store” in the playground, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Please don’t enter secrets, credentials or real personal data into the playground — use made-up identifiers like user:anne.

Share links

“Share” builds a link that contains the whole store (model, tuples, assertions and the current query), compressed into the part of the URL after #. Browsers do not send that part to the web server, so the content is not uploaded or stored by this site. But anyone who has the link can read everything in it, and wherever you paste it (chat apps, tickets, email) may store it. Treat a share link like the data inside it.
