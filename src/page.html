How the playground works

    

      

        
Where your data lives

        
    
          
  • Saved automatically, in this browser only. Every store, model, tuple and assertion is kept in this browser’s local storage on this device. There is no account and no server copy.
    • 
          
  • Nothing here touches a real OpenFGA server. “Write model”, “Write” and “delete” imitate the OpenFGA APIs on your local copy.
    • 
          
  • Other browsers and devices don’t see it. A private window, another browser or another laptop starts empty.
    • 
          
  • Clearing this site’s data deletes your stores. To keep or move one, use Share (a link) or the store export in the inspector.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Sharing

        
    
          
  • Share copies a link with a snapshot of this store — model, tuples, assertions and the current query — packed into the link itself.
    • 
          
  • Opening a shared link never changes the sender’s store. It creates your own copy in your browser. Your edits stay with you; theirs stay with them.
    • 
          
  • A link doesn’t update. If you change the store, Share again and send the new link.
    • 
          
  • Anyone with the link can read everything in it. Don’t put secrets or real personal data in a shared store.
